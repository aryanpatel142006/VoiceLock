# Adapting the separator and the pick to Aryan + Joseph (research, Oct 10)
Scope: ~12 min of clean single-speaker speech per person on the demo mic, plus 2x20 s held-out takes, and a few A100 hours. No repo edited. Sources are in brackets ([S#] at the bottom). "Estimate" marks numbers nobody measured. Calibration and longer scoring context are already in `RESEARCH_oct10_calibration.md`, so they are not repeated here.

## Repo facts that shape the options
- `hearing/tse/blind.py` builds `MossFormer2_SS_16K(args)` and `load_state_dict(strict=True)` from `_checkpoint()` [R1]. A fine-tuned checkpoint with the same architecture loads with no code change, only a path knob. MPS latency is unchanged (same 55.7 M-param net [S2]).
- ClearerVoice (Apache-2.0 [S1]) ships fine-tuning for this model: `train/speech_separation/train.sh` with `init_checkpoint_path=.../MossFormer2_SS_16K/last_best_checkpoint.pt`. The config sets `finetune_learning_rate: 0.00005`, `max_length: 2` (s), `batch_size: 1`, `effec_batch_size: 4` and `max_epoch: 120`. Data is an scp of `mix s1 s2` wav paths [S1].
- Its loss is SI-SNR wrapped in `PitWrapper` (permutation-invariant) [S1]. The two output slots are separate; PIT only decides which reference each slot is scored against. So a fixed slot order (slot 0 = Aryan) is a one-function loss change.

## 1. Fine-tuning the separator on the two voices
- **Closest modern evidence (TGIF, WASPAA 2025)** specialised a generalist TSE model to a known "talker group" (e.g. a family) [S3]. With clean targets ("oracle") it gained **+2.06 to +2.10 dB SI-SDRi** overall. With teacher pseudo-targets it gained **+1.08 to +1.26 dB**, and **+2.4 to +3.4 dB** with 3 to 5 talkers [S3]. Setup: ~50 h of 10 s mixtures across 20 groups (20 h adapt, 10 h val, 20 h eval), Adam at 1e-5, up to 120 epochs [S3].
- **Few-shot personalisation (Sivaraman & Kim, JSTSP)** fine-tuned Conv-TasNet generalists on target clean speech [S4]. 5 s gave no gain; 30 s gave +0.27 to +0.62 dB SDR. It converged in ~5-6 k mixtures at lr 1e-4 [S4]. Test sets were 100 mixtures per speaker from held-out utterances and unseen noise at -5..5 dB [S4]. We have ~24x more speech per person (12 min vs 30 s), so expect more headroom than this. That is an inference, not a measurement.
- **Speaker-dependent (fixed-order) separation** is the classic setting: the same speakers in train and test, each output tied to one speaker [S5]. A DRNN trained this way beat speaker-dependent NMF by **2.3 to 5.0 dB SDR** [S6]. Wang & Chen report that target-dependent separation comes close to speaker-dependent [S5]. For us, the pick becomes the separator's output order, learned with full mixture context instead of judged on 1 s separated crops.
- **Silent slots** (one speaker or noise only) break SI-SDR. Use MixIT's thresholded SNR, tau = 1e-3 (30 dB), and its L0 term `10log10(|y_hat|^2 + tau|x|^2)` for all-zero references [S7]. The paper reports that L0 slightly lowers 2-source SI-SNRi but improves single-source cases [S7].
- **Forgetting / generality.** Full fine-tuning gives the best target-task score but forgets the original task. LoRA (+6-12% parameters) stays competitive and keeps the original ability [S8]. Fine-tuned models beat from-scratch training by 0.29-1.8 dB [S8]. For a demo knob, keep the base checkpoint as the default, so the fine-tuned one does not need to stay general.
- **Overfitting risk.** All source audio comes from one mic and a few sessions. When train and validation share speakers, overfitting is hard to see [S9], so validate on a separate take and test on the separate session.
- **Cost (estimate):** data rendering < 0.5 h on CPU; 1-2 A100 h per run; two runs (fixed-order and PIT twin) fit in about 3-4 GPU h. Measure the step time in the first 100 steps before trusting this. **Can ship Sunday behind a knob:** yes, a checkpoint path (`HEARING_SEP_CKPT`) plus `HEARING_PICK=fixed`, both default off.

## 2. Adapting the pick (embedding model or head) on separated crops
- **What helps most is training on the separated domain.** Adding extracted speech to the verification training pool: EER 10.40 to 7.77% on overlapped speech [S10] (already in the calibration note). Retraining the head on crops cut from all 12 min (not the 60 s profile) is the cheapest next step.
- **Quality-aware calibration:** logistic regression on the score plus quality measures (speech duration, imposter-cohort mean). **About 11% relative EER** on top of a fine-tuned model, e.g. VoxSRC-20 val 2.89 to 2.59% [S11]. Our analogues: crop speech duration, stream energy ratio, and the score against a cohort of other voices.
- **Adapting the embedding network:** with little data, SE/BN adapters (88 K params, frozen backbone) beat full fine-tuning (8 M). With 50 speakers: EER 4.62 to 2.69% (adapter) vs 3.08% (full) [S12]. The authors state that full fine-tuning "often leads to severe overfitting" with scarce data [S12]. ECAPA has SE and BN layers, so the method maps onto it (inference). The catch: 2 classes cannot train an AAM-softmax embedding, so mix in many other speakers' separated crops. Estimate: 1-2 GPU h; medium risk.
- **Domain-adversarial / PLDA adaptation** targets large unlabeled domains. CORAL+ PLDA: SRE16 EER 9.69 to 6.62% [S13]; ADDA: 9.33 to 7.65% [S14]. With two *labelled* speakers, a supervised back end on separated crops is the direct equivalent. Not worth it before Sunday.
- **Use both enrollments.** TSE models also "confuse" targets. A post-filter that compares the output against target *and* interferer embeddings added +0.3 to +0.4 dB SI-SDRi; metric-learning training added about +0.6 dB [S15]. This supports a closed-set A-vs-J decision over a one-voice threshold.

## 3. Test-time adaptation and enrollment-aware separation
- **Self-training without clean targets** (RemixIT, MixIT adaptation) exists for when clean in-domain speech is missing [S16][S17]. We have clean targets. TGIF shows clean-target adaptation beating pseudo-target adaptation (+2.06 vs +1.26 dB) [S3], so supervised fine-tuning (section 1) dominates. Skip TTA.
- **Prepending the enrollment (LExt):** `[enroll; 32 ms zeros; mixture]` into an unmodified TF-GridNet, trained, gave 24.1 dB SI-SDRi on WSJ0-2mix and 18.3 dB on WHAM! [S18]. Enrollments of 0.25-0.5 s were already strong [S18]. A training-free version on our PIT separator (read the slot that holds the enrollment) is an untested hypothesis. It also doubles the input per hop, which hurts MPS latency. Not for Sunday.
- **Ready-made TSE checkpoints don't fit:** ClearerVoice's audio-only SpEx+ is trained on WSJ0-2mix at 8 kHz [S19]; USEF-TSE has code but lists no checkpoint [S20].

## 4. Evaluating fairly
- Split by **session/take, never by random crops.** Train on 4 takes per person and validate on a 5th (e.g. free talk). Test only on the 2x20 s held-out session takes, mixed with noise files used nowhere else. This mirrors S4's held-out utterances plus unseen noise [S4] and S9's call for unmatched test sets [S9].
- Synthetic mixes overstate real performance: ~20 dB SI-SNR on WSJ0-2mix vs 2.8 dB on real REAL-M recordings [S21]. Also record 2-3 live overlap takes on the demo mic. Score them with wrong-speaker time, and optionally the reference-free REAL-M SI-SNR estimator (Apache-2.0 on HF [S22]).
- 20 s per person gives about 40 one-second windows per voice: report counts and bootstrap intervals, not single means. Use SI-SDRi [S23] plus the existing wrong-speaker audit. Run base vs fine-tuned in the same harness, on all 4 target/condition rows.
- **Generality check** (PIT variant only): score base vs fine-tuned on a non-team pair (grid-s1/grid-s4 profiles or LibriSpeech test-clean, CC BY 4.0 [S24]). Any drop means "team preset only".

## Ranked top 3
1. **Fine-tune MossFormer2_SS_16K on synthetic Aryan+Joseph mixtures with fixed slot order (plus a PIT twin as the control).** It attacks the pick error at its source and has the best-backed gain (+2.1 dB with clean targets [S3]). It reuses the shipped training code [S1] and needs no inference change [R1]. About 3-4 A100 h (estimate).
2. **Retrain the closed-set pick on separated crops from all 12 min, with quality features** (crop duration, energy ratio, cohort mean) [S10][S11][S15]. CPU only, about 2 h (estimate), low risk. Do it on the #1 checkpoint's outputs if #1 ships.
3. **SE/BN adapter on ECAPA, trained on separated crops of the team plus about 200 LibriSpeech speakers** [S12][S24]. 1-2 GPU h (estimate). Medium risk; only if #2 still leaves both streams scoring "Aryan".

## Recipe for #1
- **Data (Mac, numpy):** sources are the clean takes only. Leave out the café-noise take, because its noise would leak into the targets. Split as in section 4. Noise: a no-speech café recording on the demo mic if one exists, else MUSAN (CC BY 4.0 [S25]) or WHAM! (CC BY-NC 4.0 [S26]), with disjoint files for train, val and test. Include other-voice babble *in the noise*, so the model learns to route third-party talkers to neither slot.
- **Mixture types:**
  - 2 s crops (matches `max_length: 2` [S1]).
  - 55%: A+J with 25-100% overlap and a relative level of -6..+6 dB.
  - 15% A only; 15% J only; 5% noise only; 10% A+J with no noise.
  - Speech-to-noise ratio 0-15 dB; overall gain -25..-5 dBFS.
  - Write `mix s1(=Aryan) s2(=Joseph)` wav triplets and an scp: 20 k train (about 11 h of mixtures) and 1 k val. Silent slots are all-zero wavs. Do not add RIRs: the takes already carry the room.
- **Train (A100):** clone ClearerVoice. Set `tr_list`/`cv_list` in `config/train/MossFormer2_SS_16K.yaml` and `gpu_id=0 n_gpu=1 init_checkpoint_path=<clearvoice MossFormer2_SS_16K/last_best_checkpoint.pt>` in `train.sh`, then `bash train.sh` [S1].
  - Run A: PIT, unchanged loss; drop single-speaker items, or apply L0 to them.
  - Run B: fixed order, swapping `get_si_snr_with_pitwrapper` for a per-slot thresholded SNR with L0 on silent slots [S7].
  - LR 5e-5 (repo default [S1]); fall back to 1e-5 (TGIF [S3]) if val loss worsens in the first 1 k steps.
  - Validate every 1 k steps, keep the best checkpoint, stop at about 10-20 k steps or 2 h (estimate).
- **Eval (Mac, accuracy only):**
  - Copy `last_best_checkpoint.pt` back. Run the live 1.0 s / 0.5 s pipeline over the held-out test mixtures and 2-3 real overlap takes, for base vs A vs B.
  - Metrics: SI-SDRi, wrong-speaker time and gated windows on all 4 rows. Run B with ECAPA as a cross-check, falling back to base when ECAPA strongly disagrees.
  - Ship B (or A) behind `HEARING_SEP_CKPT` / `HEARING_PICK=fixed`, default off, only if it beats base on every row. Then one live test.
