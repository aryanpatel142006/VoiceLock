# Confidence gating in live systems: T-300 research scan

Research date: 2026-10-10. Audience: muse-c and T-285 rehearsal owner. Documentation only; no runtime defaults changed. Local implementation inspected at `3ffc2a0`.

## Recommendation

**Use Option B, “Duck to room baseline” (`policy=duck`), as the provisional rehearsal starting point.** Preserve the selected identity, abstain from promoting an uncertain stem, retain a bounded room feed, and make uncertainty visible. Compare against A and C on the same recordings before adopting a stage default. This is an engineering recommendation supported by adjacent evidence, **not a published A/B victory for B**: this scan found no direct controlled study of speaker-confidence-triggered silence versus best-guess target playback.

Letter mapping is provisional: the task lists best-guess / duck / pass-through in that order, while the local rehearsal implementation uses names, not A/B/C. No board text establishing the letters was supplied or found locally. **The authoritative recommendation is the named `duck` policy**, regardless of board lettering. Local mappings and the current `stream` default are in [rehearsal.py](../../hearing/server/rehearsal.py).

Why B: conference automixing explicitly preserves room continuity [S1, S2]; hearing-aid preference evidence favors omnidirectional processing when directional benefit is marginal [S3]; real-time captions can preserve content while withholding identity [S6]. Together these support a room fallback without an asserted target identity. None establishes our optimal attenuation or proves that listeners prefer it over A. Strong differences between listeners argue for a reversible choice and blind listening [S4].

**Do not equate B with digital silence or proven target rejection.** In this checkout, a gated enrolled window returns zero target samples unless the two-profile best-guess override applies; confidence is zero. With aligned mixture supplied, the mixer then retains room audio. With confidence processing enabled and environment overrides unset, its confidence-zero floor is 6 dB attenuation, so B is approximately `0.501 × aligned_mix` after settling, at the rehearsal's 0.8 suppression. That feed includes the other speaker. The current 6 dB setting does not itself establish the project's target-absent leak requirement of at most −12 dB. [Local: enrolled.py, mixer.py, PLAN.md]

## What the targeted hunts establish

### (a) Gated silence versus best guess

No direct A/B or user-study result for that exact comparison was located. Searches covered target/speaker uncertainty, hearing-aid directionality and noise-reduction preference, conference automix manuals, live diarization, and assistant barge-in documentation. The strongest nearby preference study favors OMNI in marginal-benefit cases [S3]; the strongest warning against a universal preference comes from individual NR preferences [S4]. Neither tests rapid cross-talk through our separator. Shure supplies a production continuity rationale rather than a controlled preference result [S1].

**Inference:** B is the best starting hypothesis because it separates “I cannot identify the target” from “the audio connection disappeared.” A remains an experimental arm, not a known inferior choice. C is the explicit Raw reference and recovery control. The local C implementation switches `ab=raw` for the whole arm; it is not an automatic fallback that activates only on uncertain hops. [Local: rehearsal.py]

### (b) Numeric thresholds and false-accept costs

The surveyed sources expose **no transferable production speaker-identity confidence threshold with a paired false-accept rate** for hearing aids, Teams or Zoom. Public numbers above belong to other classifiers or controls. Lex's example 0.40 is NLU; Nuance's 0.5 is detection sensitivity, not a calibrated probability [S8, S10, S11]. Nuance documents the concrete failure tradeoff: accidental interruption versus missed onset [S8]. A numerical economic cost or false-accept rate is not supplied.

For our system, define false acceptance as audible promotion of a non-target stem while the target is absent; separately measure intelligible non-target leakage through the room fallback. B can improve the first without satisfying the second. This distinction follows the target-silent guard and leak criteria in [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md). Do not transplant NLU/VAD thresholds into cosine, LDA or projection scores.

The current confidence scale uses margins 0.54 / 0.58 fitted on a GRID stand-in, according to [CONTRACTS.md, Pick confidence](../CONTRACTS.md#pick-confidence-and-the-residual-floor-telemetrytse-t-134). Those are normalization margins, not independently validated real-room false-accept thresholds. Calibrate entry/exit cutoffs against session-disjoint target-present, target-absent and unknown-speaker recordings. Report false accepts, retained target words and recovery delay together; no threshold is recommended as evidence-backed before those measurements.

### (c) Attack/release users tolerate

**No quantified listener-tolerance bound for confidence-driven target ducking was found.** The available engineering precedents span 4 ms mic activation / 500 ms decay with 400 ms hold [S2] and 100 ms attenuation / 600 ms recovery for music ducking [S15]. These describe different gain directions and should not be averaged into a universal setting. ReSound's binaural mode switching is also not a target-stem gate [S5].

For a later envelope experiment, use **30 ms fade to room baseline, 100 ms fade back to verified target, and a 200 ms identity-lock grace period** as explicitly unvalidated starting values. Compare fade-out 30/100 ms and fade-back 100/300 ms on recorded cross-talk. The 30 ms anchor comes from the existing local mixer's ramp, not a human tolerance finding [Local: mixer.py]. Grace means retaining the selected person's identity, **not continuing an unverified stream**, and it must not override explicit negative-speaker evidence. This proposal is not implemented by this document.

A 200 ms pause also cannot be diagnosed solely by a 30 ms output ramp: T-285's local balanced preset advertises a 1 s window and 0.5 s hop, versus speed's 0.5 s / 0.25 s. Those are configuration values, not measured end-to-end delay; the room transition remains constrained by when uncertainty becomes observable. [Local: rehearsal.py] Report detection delay, decision hold, crossfade duration, transport delay and reacquisition separately.

## Concrete rehearsal handoff and stop gates

1. Start with named B (`duck`), ECAPA, balanced, suppression 0.8, matching existing matrix dimensions except policy. Record all confidence/gated-floor environment overrides and AGC settings. These are experimental choices, not vendor-derived optimal values. Run one rehearsal session per server because policy updates currently write a process-wide environment variable. [Local: rehearsal.py]
2. Use the same saved input for A/B/C, then repeat live through the intended mic/headphones. Include alternating turns, sustained overlap, a scripted 200 ms pause, target absent with other speaker active, and an unknown third speaker; swap talker positions. Freeze profiles, representation, separator, input gain and timing preset across policy comparisons. Do not change envelope and identity threshold in the same comparison.
3. Save output and pre-AGC audio plus per-hop selected identity, confidence, gate/pause state, mixture/stem levels, environment values, revision and timestamps. Inspect the final output: extractor zeros alone cannot prove audible silence [Local: enrolled.py, mixer.py]. Use delay-aligned target-active and target-absent regions; report word retention/WER, wrong-speaker time, non-target leak, transition counts and p50/p95 fallback/reacquisition delay with device and backend.
4. Randomize paired A/B playback order; match target-active playback level, but retain each policy's actual pause/room attenuation. Ask the wearer about intelligibility, wrong-person intrusions, connection-loss impression and overall preference. Two teammates can choose a rehearsal setting; they cannot establish population or clinical preference. Keep C available as the Raw reference.
5. Apply the existing project gates: target-absent non-target leak ≤−12 dB, other-dominant output ≤5%, no new lock while only the other person talks; report recovery delay [PLAN.md, Success criteria](../../PLAN.md). The unmodified confidence-zero 6 dB room floor may fail this. If B fails, test a deeper bounded room floor on the same captures and recheck word retention; do not claim a successful strict Focus mode from a preference vote alone. If no arm meets the gate, use explicitly labeled Raw/Open or the disclosed replay backup required by PLAN, not an asserted isolated target.

No new models, dependencies, runtime measurements or benchmark rows are introduced. SI-SDRi, WER, RTF and device latency were not measured by this research task. The numerical timings above are source specifications, local configuration values or labeled experiment proposals.
