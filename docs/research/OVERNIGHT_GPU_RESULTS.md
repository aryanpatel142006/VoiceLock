### 3.2 Speaker Representation & Selection (ECAPA-TDNN)

Data from T-212 evaluation across crop durations and LDA vs Cosine scoring:

- **Pair Orthogonality:**
  - Aryan vs Joseph profile cosine: **0.0658** (well below `SAME_VOICE = 0.70`).
  - Public 1320 vs 6930 profile cosine: **0.0854**.
- **Crop Duration Ladder:**
  - **0.5 s:** High intra-speaker variance; margin compresses under noise and separation cross-talk. Vulnerable to wrong-speaker flips.
  - **1.0 s:** Adequate for clean stems; marginal stability at $-10\text{ dB}$ SIR.
  - **2.0 s:** High stability across all conditions; median margin $\Delta \ge 0.45$.
  - **4.0 s:** Ceiling stability; negligible gain over 2.0 s.
- **Route Validation:**
  - Both registered team profiles activate `cand.scoring == "lda"`.
  - Open-set or un-registered buffers safely fall back to `cand.scoring == "cosine"`.
