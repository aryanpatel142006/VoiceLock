# Short-window speaker embedding robustness — T-298

Primary-source scan, 2026-10-10; bounded meeting preparation for muse-c. **Research only: no runtime, profile, configuration, model or hardware changes.** Rankings below are priorities for an offline experiment, not a benchmark leaderboard.

## Findings that change the next experiment

- Sub-second robustness is a real research topic, but **the exact ECAPA cosine-margin curve 0.45 → 0.15 was not found in the inspected papers**. EER, classification accuracy, mean pairwise cosine separation and target-minus-negative profile cosine are different quantities. Do not substitute one for another.
- UtterIdNet is the clearest retrieved sub-second result: **EER 6.46% at 0.5 s versus 6.33% at 2 s**, with 6.88% at 0.25 s and 6.41% at 1 s (VoxCeleb2 training, VoxCeleb1 testing, [P1, Table 2](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2019/hajavi19_interspeech.pdf)). This is a different encoder and protocol, not a promised improvement to our ECAPA checkpoint.
- Published embedding-memory weights exist: Sholokhov et al. compare **new-observation weight α=0.1** with **α_t=1/(t+1)** on 2 s chunks, including unknown guests [P2, §4, §6.2, Table 2]. Their online enrollment adaptation is not a validated 0.5 s separator-stem smoother.
- We already have short-crop, aggregation, projection, metric and EMA experiments. The most important local result is **T-341's reversal on separated speech**, not the large clean-audio margin in T-305. Reuse those reports and caches before creating another projection head.

## Alternatives to the 192 → 64 → 32 head

1. **Immutable full enrollment + raw cosine**, calibrated for duration and target absence, remains the control. T-341 found it better than the head on separated streams. Centering/whitening is an offline ablation, not a reason to replace the working path.
2. **Cosine + AS-norm** adjusts a trial score relative to enrollment/test impostor-cohort score distributions. It changes score calibration, not the embedding itself. P3 gives actual cohort sizes; a cohort of only our two teammates is not equivalent. Development-only cohort selection and independent evaluation are required. Normalized scores need new thresholds; raw cosine floors cannot be reused.
3. **Spherical PLDA** (P2) is particularly relevant to a small household: constrained covariance and count-aware multiple-enrollment scoring instead of a two-class nonlinear projection. Full PLDA (P13) needs reliable multi-speaker within/between-speaker statistics; two speakers from one session each cannot estimate that robustly. Counts of overlapping crops are not independent enrollment observations.
4. **NPLDA** (P10) directly learns pair verification scores rather than projecting a vector then using cosine. Its training objective better matches accept/reject decisions, but the retrieved experiment is not short-window evidence. [T-302's source audit](BORROWED_EMBEDDING.md) found no license file in the inspected NeuralPlda tree: inspect permissions before copying code. No code or model is introduced here.
5. **Long-to-short embedding distillation** (P5/P6) attacks duration mismatch without asking a two-person loss to push all negatives antipodal. An offline candidate could use matched clean longer teacher context and short student crops, with frozen-teacher anchoring. This is a proposed transfer experiment, not a reported result for frozen ECAPA or for the existing head.

## Temporal memory: published weights versus our prototypes

P2 §4 uses `c_next = α*x + (1-α)*c` only when the winning score exceeds a development-tuned threshold. **α means new observation weight.** Table 2 tests α=.1 and α_t=1/(t+1). Those are per accepted **utterance**, not per 10 ms audio hop. On 2 s chunks, its SpeechBrain cosine EER changes from 4.42/4.44 to 3.53/3.57 for known/unknown non-targets (ASVspoof protocol). This is evidence for thresholded profile adaptation, not evidence that a free-running rolling separator centroid is safe.

Our [T-275 buffer](EMA_CROP_BUFFER.md) uses old-state decay `2^(-elapsed_s/half_life_s)`, half-life 1 s. Derived new weights are .2929 at a .5 s hop and .5 at a 1 s hop; these are arithmetic translations of the local formula, **not published optimal weights**. Our [T-282 tracker](DYNAMIC_EMA_TRACKER.md) uses new weight .02–.35 at a 1 s reference hop, then time-scales it. These defaults are uncalibrated and its favorable result is synthetic; immutable matching performed equally well on accepted selection.

For future evaluation: keep enrollment immutable, update separate state only on accepted identity evidence, associate identities across stem permutations, freeze on pauses/ambiguity, and reset on explicit target/model changes. Measure switch delay and rejected-target duration as well as jitter. A smoother that plays a wrong identity longer fails even if its cosine trace looks cleaner. Correlated overlapping hops do not create additional independent speech.

## Steal list: top five bounded offline experiments

1. **Reuse the full-enrollment control and T-341's separated/open-set trials.** Compare raw cosine, development-centered cosine and duration-calibrated rejection on identical cached candidates. Include target absent, unknown voice, quiet speech and stem permutations. Primary motivation: P2's guest protocol; local gate: beat T-341 raw without worse target rejection/wrong acceptance. No new head before this baseline is pinned.
2. **Add AS-norm as a score-only ablation**, with a consented/public disjoint multi-speaker cohort in a future authorized task. P3 supplies the recipe; T-302 already audited relevant code. Report cohort size and duration matching. Reject if it helps pooled EER but worsens held-out absent-target accepts. Do not download data or alter thresholds in this task.
3. **Compare raw/no-memory, accepted-window running mean and gated EMA α=.1**, translating by actual hop duration and documenting the convention. P2 supplies published weights; reuse T-275/T-282 mechanics instead of rebuilding. Keep an immutable anchor and log rejected updates. Require better identity behavior with acceptable reacquisition delay; the T-275 .5 s result currently fails this gate.
4. **Compare crop-pair score averaging with embedding averaging** using only causal past voiced context, plus a longer-window control. P4's TTA-3 improves where TTA-2 worsens; P5 motivates learned aggregation if the cheap control works. Match available audio and compute across variants; correlated crops and extra acquisition time must be visible. This transfer to .5 s hops is unvalidated.
5. **Use long-to-short teacher alignment before a new two-class projection architecture.** P5/P6 supply the loss idea; compare a frozen-teacher, pretrained-anchored candidate against the existing head using session-disjoint data when available. Measure verification EER/FAR and separated-stream correctness rather than optimizing only intra−cross cosine. Until new sessions exist, record results as within-session research only.
